Devotional reels compete with everything on the feed. The blessing is that bhakti emotion is already powerful — your job is simply to not bury it.

## The hooks that perform

- Open on the aarti flame, not the logo
- Start mid-chant; add context later
- One bold on-screen shloka in the first second
- Temple bells as the audio hook
- A slow push-in instead of fast cuts
- Ask a devotional question ("कोणत्या गावाचा गणपती?")
- End on the deity's eyes, loop-friendly

## Watch time is devotion time

Reels that hold past 7 seconds get pushed by the algorithm. That is why we front-load the most sacred frame, not the most branded one.

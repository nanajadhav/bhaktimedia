The devotee of 2026 discovers temples on YouTube, confirms timings on Google, and donates through UPI. Trusts that treat digital as seva — not an afterthought — see footfall and donations rise together.

## The five pillars

- **Live darshan** — weekly aarti livestreams with clean audio
- **Festival campaigns** — posters, reels and WhatsApp forwards started 21 days before the utsav
- **A living website** — timings, history, donation page and virtual seva
- **Consistent identity** — one logo, one colour system, one voice
- **Community replies** — answering every comment within 24 hours

## Start small, stay consistent

One livestream a week beats ten videos in a festival panic. Devotees return to channels that feel like a daily ritual.

A devotional poster is not an advertisement — it is a small temple on paper. When a devotee looks at it at 5 AM before aarti, the design must feel calm, sacred and alive.

## Colour carries emotion

Saffron signals energy and sacrifice, gold carries prosperity and divinity, and deep sandalwood grounds the composition in earth. We always begin a poster with a **three-colour discipline** — no more.

## Typography is tone of voice

Devanagari headlines deserve classical serifs with generous matras, while event details stay in clean geometric type. Mixing them carelessly is the most common mistake we fix for temples.

> ॥ सौंदर्यं भक्तिभावः ॥ — beauty itself becomes devotion when intent is pure.

## Our checklist before delivery

- Deity imagery respectful and scripturally accurate
- Hierarchy: deity → festival name → date → venue
- Print-safe margins and CMYK profile
- Matching WhatsApp-forward version at 1080×1920

Every poster we ship passes a review with temple advisors before it reaches your mandir's wall.

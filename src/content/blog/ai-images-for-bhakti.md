AI can produce a thousand deity images a day — but only a handful are fit for a temple wall. The difference is curation, culture and constraint.

## A prompt that works

We structure every devotional prompt in four layers: subject, mood, light, and tradition.

> "Shri Ganesh at golden hour, soft temple bokeh, marigold petals, cinematic rim light, traditional Peshwai palette, devotional poster style, 4K."

## Our ethics rules

- No training on copyrighted temple photography without consent
- Iconography reviewed against scriptural references
- Human artists refine hands, mudras and ornaments — AI still struggles here
- AI-generated images are always disclosed to clients

## Where AI shines

Backgrounds, textures, cosmic scenes and festival mood boards — freeing our illustrators to focus on the deity itself.

The result: divine speed with human reverence.

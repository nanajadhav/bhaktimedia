Every temple, trust, and devotional creator has different needs — a livestream that never buffers, a poster that gets shared a thousand times, a channel that finally breaks past its plateau. Below is the full range of what we do, and how each service actually helps.

## Devotional Posters

Festival posters that devotees actually save and print — not just scroll past. We design category-wise: Ganesh Utsav, Navratri, Diwali, jayantis, and more, each built around the specific deity's traditional colors, symbols, and mood. A complete identity system for your mandir — signage, trust letterheads, event branding, print collateral, and digital assets that all feel like they belong to the same sacred space. Built once, used for years, consistent across every touchpoint a devotee encounters.

End-to-end channel care so your trust or committee doesn't have to become a media team overnight. We handle content planning, editing, thumbnail design, SEO, livestream production, and community growth — the kind of steady, patient work that turns an 8K-subscriber channel into a 240K one.

Available live in the AI Studio, in any size you need.

## Video Editing

Cinematic aarti films, utsav aftermovies, and devotional edits paced with the same reverence as the moment itself. We don't treat sacred footage like generic content — the pacing, music, and cuts are built to hold the feeling of actually being there.

## Bhakti Reels

Scroll-stopping vertical content built for how devotees actually watch today — strong hooks, clean transitions, and trending devotional audio, without losing the soul of what's being shown. Made for Instagram and YouTube Shorts alike.

## Voiceovers

Warm, studio-grade narration in Marathi, Hindi, Sanskrit, and English. Whether it's a katha narration, an aarti introduction, or a temple documentary, tone matters as much as translation — we match the voice to the occasion.

## Thumbnail Design

High-CTR thumbnails tuned specifically for spiritual audiences and how YouTube's algorithm surfaces devotional content. Clear faces, legible text, warm contrast — small design decisions that make a measurable difference in click-through rate.

## Social Media

Content calendars, captions, and day-to-day community management for temples and creators who don't have hours to spend on Instagram each week. We keep the posting consistent so the growth is too.

Fast, SEO-ready websites for temples and devotional creators, built on modern stacks. Whether it's a donation page, an event calendar, or a full digital home for your trust, we build sites that load quickly and rank well — because a temple's website is often a devotee's first darshan.

# Website Design & Development

॥ यत्र द्वारं तत्र दर्शनम् ॥

For many devotees today, the website is the first darshan — before the aarti, before the visit, before the donation. We build temple and creator websites that load fast, feel sacred, and actually convert visitors into devotees, donors, and subscribers.

---

## What We Build

### Temple Websites
A digital home for your mandir — history, timings, live aarti streaming, event calendars, photo galleries, and a donation flow that's simple enough for a 70-year-old devotee and secure enough for a trust to fully rely on.

### Creator & Channel Websites
A central hub that ties together your YouTube, Instagram, and reels — portfolio-style galleries, blog integration, and a clean way for brands or committees to reach out for collaborations.

### Donation & Seva Portals
Secure, fast, mobile-first donation pages with clear seva categories (annadan, gaushala, festival sponsorship, etc.), receipts, and recurring-donation support — built to reduce drop-off at the exact moment someone decides to give.

### Event & Utsav Microsites
Dedicated pages for major festivals and utsavs — schedules, live links, sponsor recognition, and photo/video uploads from the event itself, live while it's happening.

## What's Included

- **Modern, fast stack** — built for speed, since slow-loading sites lose devotees before they even see the homepage
- **SEO from day one** — structured so your temple or channel actually ranks for the searches devotees are making
- **Mobile-first design** — most devotees will visit from a phone, often during the aarti itself
- **Multilingual support** — Marathi, Hindi, Sanskrit and English content, without breaking the layout
- **Livestream integration** — embed YouTube livestreams directly, with schedule reminders
- **Analytics & handover** — you get full visibility into traffic and a simple way to update content yourselves, no developer needed for day-to-day changes

## How It Works

**॥ ०१ ॥ Sankalp — Discover**
We understand your temple's story, your existing digital presence, and what devotees actually need from the site.

**॥ ०२ ॥ Srishti — Create**
Design and development happen in parallel — you see working pages, not just mockups, within the first week.

**॥ ०३ ॥ Manthan — Refine**
You review on real devices, we refine copy, layout, and speed until it feels right.

**॥ ०४ ॥ Utsav — Launch**
We launch, connect your domain, set up analytics, and stay on for support as traffic grows.

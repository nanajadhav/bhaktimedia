# Digital Marketing for Temples & Devotional Creators

॥ यशो लभते धर्मात् ॥

Great devotional content deserves to actually reach people. We handle the strategy, distribution, and growth work that turns beautiful posters and heartfelt reels into real reach — more devotees at your utsav, more subscribers on your channel, more donors for your seva.

---

## What We Do

### Social Media Marketing
Platform-specific strategy for Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube — content calendars built around your festival and event calendar, not generic templates. We know when Ekadashi traffic spikes and when Ganesh Utsav content needs to go live.

### Paid Campaigns
Targeted ad campaigns for temple events, fundraising drives, and channel growth — built to reach devotees in the right city, the right language, and the right moment (like the week before a major utsav).

### SEO & Search Growth
Making sure your temple or channel shows up when devotees search for a specific aarti, a specific katha, or "temples near me" — technical SEO, content SEO, and local search optimization for temples with a physical location.

### Community & Engagement Management
Responding to comments, messages, and WhatsApp inquiries with the warmth devotees expect from a temple — not generic customer-support replies.

### Analytics & Reporting
Clear, non-technical reporting so your trust or committee actually understands what's working — which posts drive donations, which reels bring new subscribers, which campaigns are worth repeating next year.

### Influencer & Creator Collaborations
Connecting temples and devotional brands with the right creators for authentic collaborations — sponsored content, event coverage, and shoutouts that feel genuine, not transactional.

## Why It Matters

A temple with a beautifully produced aarti livestream that only 40 people see hasn't reached its potential. The content is half the work — getting it in front of the right devotees, consistently, is the other half. That's the gap digital marketing closes.
